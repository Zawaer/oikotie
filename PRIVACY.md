# Privacy policy — Oikotie

*Last updated 2026-09-10*

Oikotie is a browser extension that automates the repetitive steps of signing in to Kampus, Nova and Studeo. This is everything it does with data.

## What is stored

Only the settings you choose: your school, its login address, your interface language, and which services are switched on. They are kept in your browser's extension storage. If you have browser sync enabled, your browser may sync them between your own devices; Oikotie itself does not.

## What is not stored

Your username and password. Oikotie never reads, stores or transmits credentials. It waits for your browser's own autofill and then presses the sign-in button you would have pressed.

## Network

Oikotie makes no network requests of its own. It contacts no server, includes no analytics, and loads no remote code. The only network activity is the login pages you were already visiting.

## Permissions

Oikotie asks for access to the login pages of Kampus, Nova, Studeo and the MPASSid proxy, plus the single school login address you pick during setup. That access is used only to click buttons and select your school on those pages.

## Third parties

No data is shared with anyone. Oikotie is not affiliated with Sanoma Pro, Otava, Studeo, MPASSid or any school or municipality.

## Source

The complete source code is public at https://github.com/Zawaer/oikotie, so all of the above can be verified.
